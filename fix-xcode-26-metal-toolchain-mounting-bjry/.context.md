## Problem
Xcode 26.6 reports the MetalToolchain asset as installed, but xcrun cannot execute metal because the Cryptex is not mounted.

## Evidence
- Xcode build: 17F113
- Metal asset build: 17F109
- xcodebuild -showComponent metalToolchain reports Status: installed
- xcrun --sdk macosx metal -v still reports missing Metal Toolchain

## Acceptance criteria
- xcrun --sdk macosx metal -v executes successfully.
- oMLX custom-kernel Homebrew installation completes with args = ["with-custom-kernel"].
- Re-enable the custom-kernel option in modules/services/omlx/darwin.nix and verify the system switch.
